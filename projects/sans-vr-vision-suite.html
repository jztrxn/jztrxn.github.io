Overview

I'm developing a suite of visual ophthalmic tests in virtual reality to better understand visual perception. Working with NASA, the goal is a VR platform for monitoring eye health in microgravity to better understand Spaceflight-Associated Neuro-Ocular Syndrome (SANS).

What I'm building

  • A visual field test in virtual reality, combined with other common ophthalmologic tests in VR to evaluate the eye health of astronauts.
  • A Vision Suite of tests being implemented in the UNR Student Outreach Clinic as a feasibility study, including IRB submission.

Presentations

Designing a Virtual-Reality Neuro-Visual Assessment Platform for Spaceflight-Associated Neuro-Ocular Syndrome. Speaker presentation at the Microgravity to Medicine Symposium, International VR Healthcare Association (IVRHA); Orlando, FL; October 27, 2026.
