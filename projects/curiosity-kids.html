Overview

Curiosity Kids was created during AWE Reality Hack 2026. Using Snap Spectacles and Lens Studio, our team built a child-centered curiosity engine that encourages children to interact with and learn from the world around them. The project was a finalist, placing in the top 4 of more than 20 competing teams.

My role

  • Background research, the white paper, and the business plan.
  • Documentation and the final presentation.
